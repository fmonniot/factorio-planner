What is real. Block content, rates, summary panes, overview rows and detail strips come from the app's own solver and summary code. Layout comes from a model calibrated on the baseline screenshots (302 px above the table on Metals at 1440 × 900); the readout is computed from what is drawn. Chips use the app's tooltip (name and exact rate). The +N on an area row opens the app's popup today; here it opens on click, not hover.
What is not drawn. The Fold 8 sizes are the ones measured on the owner's device (cover 416 × 657, inner 816 × 616); the iPad A16 is its published 820 × 1180. Machine chips show ×count on a grey plate, not the machine icon. Pickers, modals, menus behind + Recipe, the tool buttons and the workspace button are not drawn: clicking them shows a note. Row reordering, drag and drop, and the light theme are not drawn. Totals are the solver's and can differ slightly from the app's toolbar.
